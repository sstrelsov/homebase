Houston Mail

Houston Mail is my personal desktop tool for reading, searching, drafting, and sending email with my local assistants. It connects my own Google and Microsoft email accounts through their APIs, with my authorization.

It also connects my personal Google Drive to read, search, download, and upload files at my request.

The mail client runs on my devices. There is no public signup or hosted mailbox service.
