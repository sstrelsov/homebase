Houston Mail

Houston Mail privacy policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Houston Mail is a personal desktop email tool operated by Spencer Strelsov. It connects his own email accounts to his local Houston command line and assistants. It has no public signup or hosted mailbox service.

Data accessed and used

With the account owner's authorization, Houston Mail accesses account identity, email messages, sender and recipient details, attachments, folders, and labels. It uses this data to read and search mail, prepare drafts, and send messages as directed. At the owner's request, it can use sent messages to create a private writing-style guide.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in macOS Keychain. Account settings, saved email files, and writing-style guides are stored on the owner's devices. Houston Mail has no central email database. Drafts and sent messages remain with the email provider; saved local files remain until the owner deletes them.

Assistants and data sharing

Houston Mail communicates with Google or Microsoft to perform email operations. When the owner asks an AI assistant to work with email, relevant content may be processed by that assistant's configured AI service and retained in its conversation records under that service's settings and policies. The owner chooses which assistants have access.

Houston Mail does not sell email data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Messages are shared with recipients when the owner directs a send. Other transfers are limited to providing the requested email features or meeting a legal obligation.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke access through their Google or Microsoft account settings. Removing an account from Houston deletes its local account configuration and Keychain credentials. Saved email files, writing-style guides, and assistant conversation records must be deleted separately. Removing Houston access does not delete mail from the email provider.

Google user data

Houston Mail follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, for Google account data and transfers of that data.

Contact and changes

Contact sstrelsov@gmail.com with questions. Changes to these practices will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
