Houston Mail privacy policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Houston Mail is a personal desktop tool operated by Spencer Strelsov. It connects his own email accounts and Google Drive to his local Houston command line and assistants. It has no public signup or hosted mailbox service.

Data accessed and used

With the account owner's authorization, Houston Mail accesses account identity, email messages, sender and recipient details, attachments, folders, and labels. It uses this data to read and search mail, prepare drafts, and send messages as directed. At the owner's request, it can use sent messages to create a private writing-style guide.

When the owner connects Google Drive, it also accesses file names, metadata, folders, and file contents to read, search, download, and export files. It uploads local files to the owner's selected Drive destination when directed. Uploaded files inherit the destination folder's access; Houston does not change file sharing settings.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in macOS Keychain. Account settings, saved email and Drive files, and writing-style guides are stored on the owner's devices. Houston Mail has no central email or file database. Drafts and sent messages remain with the email provider; uploaded files remain in Google Drive. Saved local files remain until the owner deletes them.

Assistants and data sharing

Houston Mail communicates with Google or Microsoft to perform email and Drive operations. When the owner asks an AI assistant to work with email or files, relevant content may be processed by that assistant's configured AI service and retained in its conversation records under that service's settings and policies. The owner chooses which assistants have access.

Houston Mail does not sell email or Drive data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Messages are shared with recipients when the owner directs a send. Other transfers are limited to providing the requested email and file features or meeting a legal obligation.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke access through their Google or Microsoft account settings. Removing an account from Houston deletes its local account configuration and Keychain credentials. Saved email and Drive files, writing-style guides, and assistant conversation records must be deleted separately. Removing Houston access does not delete mail or files from their providers.

Google user data

Houston Mail follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, for Google account data and transfers of that data.

Contact and changes

Contact sstrelsov@gmail.com with questions. Changes to these practices will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
